Senior Data Scientist

We are looking for a Senior Data Scientist to join our AI team at TechInnovate. In this role, you will lead data-driven decision making and develop machine learning models that power our core products.

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement machine learning models for predictive analytics and recommendation systems
- Collaborate with engineering teams to deploy models to production
- Analyze large datasets to extract actionable business insights
- Mentor junior data scientists and contribute to technical strategy
- Communicate findings to stakeholders through visualizations and presentations

Requirements:
- 5+ years of experience in data science or related field
- Strong programming skills in Python (pandas, numpy, scikit-learn, tensorflow/pytorch)
- Experience with SQL and big data technologies (Spark, Hadoop)
- Solid understanding of statistics and machine learning algorithms
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, or related field (Master's/PhD preferred)

Preferred:
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Knowledge of NLP or computer vision
- Published research or contributions to open source projects
